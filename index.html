Kernel Run

Deliver the data packet from Download_Manager to the Backup_Drive (D:) drop zone. You can't carry it — drag a window sideways too fast and it slips off. Flick a window upward to launch the packet through the air, across the Firewall (windows can't cross it), and catch it on the far side. Close windows (x) before the leaking RAM hits 100% and triggers a GC sweep.
